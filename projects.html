Private Finance Dashboard

A local, privacy-first budgeting app that reads bank statements (CSV, Excel, PDF) straight from your computer — no cloud, no bank login, nothing ever uploaded. It parses statements from four different banks across two countries, categorizes spending, and tracks savings goals.

The part I'm most proud of: a monthly spending forecast built on least-squares linear regression per category, trained only on completed months so the current partial month doesn't bias the trend. It falls back to a plain average when the fit is too weak (r² < 0.3) or history is too short — a small but deliberate choice to avoid presenting a shaky trend as a confident number.

PikoGPT — A Compact GPT Built From Scratch

A 37M-parameter decoder-only transformer trained from scratch — no pre-trained weights, no fine-tuning an existing model. Our team of four ("DropOuters") built the full pipeline end to end: data cleaning, tokenization, pre-training, and supervised fine-tuning.

The model runs 11 layers with 8 attention heads (4 KV heads), a 384 embedding dimension, and a 1,024-token context window, pre-trained on 2.2B tokens of OpenWebText on a single GPU. I worked on the post-training stage, where we compared two SFT strategies — one optimizing for broad benchmark coverage, one using language-model replay to prevent catastrophic forgetting.

The headline finding: supervised fine-tuning improved the model's task awareness but not its reasoning depth, and dataset balance mattered more than dataset size at this parameter count.
