About

A bit about me

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of St. Gallen, focused on data science, machine learning, and systems engineering. My work sits at the intersection of applied AI and finance, from automating trading workflows to building tools that make sense of everyday financial data.

Originally from Baku, Azerbaijan, I moved to Switzerland for my Master's after completing a B.Sc. in Information Technologies. Along the way I've worked in trading IT, technical infrastructure, and large-scale event operations. That experience taught me as much about coordinating under pressure as it did about writing code.

Most recently I built a 37M-parameter language model from scratch with a team of four for the PikoGPT Challenge: architecture, pre-training, and fine-tuning, no shortcuts. I speak Azerbaijani, English, Turkish, and Russian.
